description: A big, traditional seaside town beach on the Moray Firth — "the Brighton of the North" — with a long sandy East Beach, a working harbour, links golf and bottlenose dolphins offshore in the firth. Dogs are welcome all year, with a couple of honest caveats — the East Beach is on-lead throughout, and the RSPB dunes at neighbouring Culbin want leads in the nesting season. A short drive from our Aviemore lodges for a Cairngorms-and-coast week.
dog_access: partial
dog_restriction: Year-round — but East Beach is on-lead
restriction_detail: Dogs are welcome on all Nairn beaches year-round — there's no seasonal ban. But the East Beach requires dogs on a lead at all times, and the neighbouring Culbin Sands RSPB reserve asks for leads from 1 April to mid-August to protect ground-nesting birds. Check the signage for which stretch you're on.
safety_note: Moray Firth tides and cold water, with currents at the harbour channel and river mouth. Dolphins offshore are a treat — but keep dogs out of the water near boats. No year-round lifeguard.
