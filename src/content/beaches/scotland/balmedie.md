name: Balmedie

location: Near Aberdeen
description: Miles of wild dunes and open sand just north of Aberdeen, in a country park — one of the great big-sky dog walks of the east coast, and dog-friendly all year. The dunes run for so long that even a busy day leaves empty sand a short walk north or south. The one thing to respect is the nesting birds — leads in the dunes in season. A day trip from our Deeside cottage, and worth it for the scale of the place.
dog_access: year-round
dog_restriction: Year-round (leads in the dunes for nesting birds in season)
restriction_detail: No seasonal ban — dogs are welcome all year and free to run on the open sand. But keep them on a lead in the dunes during the ground-nesting-bird season (roughly spring to mid-summer) to protect the birds. Check the signage at the country park.
safety_note: An exposed North Sea beach with a strong tide and cold water, and dunes extensive enough to lose sight of the sea in — keep your bearings. No year-round lifeguard.
