name: Rockcliffe

location: Near Dalbeattie
description: A small, pretty, rocky cove on the Colvend coast, linked to neighbouring Kippford by the Jubilee Path, with a walk out to Rough Island at low tide. Dog-friendly year-round like all of Galloway, and gentler and more sheltered than the open Solway sands — a good one for a quieter or more nervous dog. National Trust for Scotland looks after the coast here, and our Galloway lodge is close by.
dog_access: year-round
dog_restriction: Year-round — no seasonal ban
restriction_detail: No seasonal dog ban (Dumfries & Galloway has none). Rough Island, reachable on foot at low tide, is a bird reserve — keep dogs on a lead there and stay off it in the nesting season (roughly May to July). Watch the tide for the crossing.
safety_note: Rough Island cuts off fast on the incoming tide — don't get stranded out there. Rocky underfoot, and the same fast Solway tides as the rest of this coast.
