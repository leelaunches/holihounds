name: Warkworth
region: northumberland
location: Near Amble
description: A long, wide sweep of sand between the Coquet estuary and Alnmouth, backed by dunes and a golf links — and dog-friendly all year. You walk in, either twenty minutes down from the village or from the golf club car park, and that small effort is exactly why it stays quiet even in August. Warkworth village itself, looped by the river beneath its castle, is one of the prettiest in Northumberland and an easy stroll with a dog afterwards.
dog_access: year-round
dog_restriction: Year-round — no seasonal ban
restriction_detail: No seasonal dog ban — dogs are welcome all year. Keep them under control around the dunes and the golf links behind the beach, and near the estuary mouth at the southern end where the Coquet runs out. Check the signage on arrival.
safety_note: The Coquet estuary at the southern end has fast channel currents that catch people out — keep dogs clear of the river mouth. Open North Sea rips and cold water on the main beach, and no year-round lifeguard. The dunes are fragile, so keep to the paths.
