location: Near Warkworth
description: A working harbour town at the mouth of the River Coquet with a long, dune-backed beach at Amble Links — dogs welcome all year, with no summer ban. A mile offshore lies Coquet Island, an RSPB reserve holding the UK's only breeding colony of roseate terns alongside puffins, kittiwakes and a resident grey seal colony; you can't land on it, but boat trips run from the harbour. The coast path runs north towards Warkworth and south past Hauxley to Druridge Bay, which makes Amble an easy base for a whole week of beach walking.
dog_access: year-round
dog_restriction: Year-round — no seasonal ban
restriction_detail: No seasonal dog ban — dogs are welcome on Amble Links all year. One thing worth knowing — the 1 May to 30 September beach restriction that sometimes turns up in searches for Amble actually belongs to Newbiggin-by-the-Sea, further down the coast, not here. The smaller town beach at Little Shore is reported inconsistently, so if you head there rather than to the Links, check the signage on arrival.
safety_note: The Coquet estuary and the harbour entrance have fast channel currents and working boat traffic — keep dogs well clear of the harbour mouth. Cold North Sea water and open-coast rips on the beach itself, and no year-round lifeguard.
